About SailVisible

A new agency, named for setting sail — and our own website is our first case study.

Why “SailVisible”

Our name comes from 扬帆启航 — “raise the sails and set off.” A business entering the age of AI search is a ship leaving harbor: the old map, the list of blue links, no longer decides who gets found. What matters now is whether the AI can find you, read you, and cite you.

Who we are

We are a small, new agency. We work with Chinese manufacturers, exporters, and traditional brands going global — the businesses that make good products and are now discovering that the overseas buyer’s first question goes to an AI, not a search box.

Our own site is our first case study

We run the exact playbook on this website that we sell to clients: open crawler access, structured data, pages written so an AI can quote them, and weekly measurement with a fixed set of prompts. The results we report to clients are the same measurements we run on ourselves. If the playbook stops working on our own site, we will know before anyone else — and we will say so.

How we work

  • Measure first. Every engagement starts with an audit and a baseline, so claims can be checked against numbers.
  • Plain language. We explain the mechanism before we sell the work. If you understand how AI search picks a product, you can judge whether we are doing it well.
  • No hype. We do not promise recommendations, rankings, or results we have not measured. Placement in AI answers is not guaranteed — by anyone — and we say that on the record.

Talk to us

If you want to know where your brand stands in AI search, write to hello@sailvisible.com. The first step is an audit; after that, we tell you plainly what we can and cannot do.
