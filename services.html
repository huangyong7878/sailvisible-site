Services

Five services, one goal: your products show up when people ask AI for them.

1. AI Visibility Audit

What it is. We check where your brand stands in AI search today. We test whether AI crawlers can reach your site, whether your pages are in the indexes that AI search draws on, and what ChatGPT and Perplexity currently say when asked about your products.

What you get.

  • A written report with findings, in plain language
  • A ranked list of fixes, starting with the ones that matter most
  • A baseline measurement — mention and citation rates you can compare against later

Every engagement starts with an audit. It is the only way to know whether later work is doing anything.

2. Technical GEO

What it is. We make your website readable by AI systems. That means allowing the AI crawlers in your robots.txt — GPTBot, which collects training data for OpenAI; OAI-SearchBot, which builds the index ChatGPT search draws on; and PerplexityBot and ClaudeBot, which fetch pages for their own answers; adding Product and Organization structured data so prices, specs, ratings, and availability are machine-readable; keeping your pages in Bing’s index, which feeds ChatGPT search; and making sure key content is served as HTML, not hidden behind JavaScript that a crawler cannot read.

What you get.

  • A corrected robots.txt and sitemap, verified live
  • Product and Organization schema on your key pages
  • Bing Webmaster Tools and Google Search Console set up, with your sitemap submitted
  • A checklist of pages the AI crawlers can and cannot reach, before and after

3. AI-Answer Content

What it is. Buying guides, comparison pages, and FAQ pages written to answer the exact questions people ask AI search — and written so an AI can quote them. That means direct answers near the top of the page, clear specs in tables, prices stated plainly, and short, self-contained paragraphs.

What you get.

  • A content plan built from the questions your customers actually ask
  • Pages your team can publish, or we can publish for you
  • Each page checked against a simple rule: can an AI copy one paragraph from it and answer a real question?

4. Third-Party Mentions

What it is. AI search trusts independent sources more than a brand’s own pages. We identify where your product should be talked about — review sites, comparison articles, forums, and communities — and help you earn mentions there with review samples, clear product information, and outreach drafts.

What you get.

  • A list of target publications and pages, ranked by relevance
  • Outreach drafts and product information packs
  • Tracking of where your brand appears, and whether those pages get cited

We do not buy placements and we do not write fake reviews. Mentions that AI systems trust are earned, not bought.

5. Monitoring

What it is. A fixed set of prompts — the questions your customers ask — run the same way every week across ChatGPT and Perplexity. Each prompt is asked several times, because AI answers vary from run to run. We record whether your brand is mentioned, whether it is cited with a link, and how it compares with competitors. Google AI Overviews is covered in the audit and in technical work; weekly prompt monitoring runs in ChatGPT and Perplexity, where answers can be tested repeatably.

What you get.

  • A weekly report with mention and citation rates, not single screenshots
  • Competitor comparison on the same prompt set
  • Recommendations for what to change next, based on what the data shows

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your site and product range, and on which services you need. Every engagement starts with an audit. Contact us for a quote.
